#### Step 4: Create a Company

In the Paperclip dashboard, click New Company

Give it a name (e.g., "Dev Team")

Set the mission: "Build and ship software on demand."

Click Save

#### Create the company

Open your Paperclip dashboard and click Create Company. The most important field is the mission statement. Your agents read this constantly. Be specific.

Bad mission statement:

> "Help me with my business."

Good mission statement:

> "Produce 5 SEO-optimized articles per week for B2B SaaS clients. Each article must be 1,500-2,500 words, pass a QA review for accuracy and brand voice, and include 3+ internal links. Never publish without explicit QA approval."

The more specific the mission, the better your agents understand what done looks like.
