# Robotics

#Engineering #STEAM-IE #Robotics

Robotics involves the design, construction, operation, and use of robots. The goal of robotics is to design machines that can help and assist humans. It integrates [[Physics]], [[Mathematics]], and [[Artificial-Intelligence]].
