# Blockchain

#Technology #STEAM-IE #Blockchain

A blockchain is a distributed ledger with growing lists of records (blocks) that are securely linked together via cryptographic hashes. See [[Cryptography]].

It forms the backbone of [[FinTech-Innovations]].
