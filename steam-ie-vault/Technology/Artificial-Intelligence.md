# Artificial Intelligence

#Technology #STEAM-IE #AI

AI is intelligence demonstrated by machines, as opposed to natural intelligence displayed by animals including humans. It relies heavily on [[Data-Science]] and [[Applied-Statistics]].

It is central to modern [[Innovation-Strategy]] and [[Venture-Capital]] funding.
