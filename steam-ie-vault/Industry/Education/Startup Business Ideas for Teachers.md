## 30 Business Ideas for Teachers (Summer & Beyond)

Transform teaching expertise into additional income streams

Teachers possess valuable skills that translate to entrepreneurship—the ability to explain complex concepts, patience, organization, and understanding how people learn. Whether you're looking for summer income, year-round supplementary earnings, or a path to a career change, these business ideas leverage what teachers do best.

## Top 5 business ideas for teachers

1

### 1\. Tutoring Service

Top Pick

Best for: Teachers looking for flexible, direct application of their subject knowledge with good hourly pay.

Pricing

$40-100/hour

Private tutoring in your subject expertise. In-person or online via Zoom. Charge $40-100/hour depending on subject and grade level. SAT/ACT prep commands premium rates. Summers and after school are prime times.

#### Pros

- Leverage existing subject expertise
- Flexible hours, choose your schedule
- High hourly rates ($40-100+)
- In-person or online options

#### Cons

- Income can be inconsistent
- Requires active client acquisition
- Limited by your available hours

Our Verdict

A classic and reliable side hustle for teachers. Start by offering services to students you know or through local networks. Online platforms can expand your reach, making it a viable year-round option.

2

### 2\. Online Course Creation

Best for: Teachers who want to package their expertise into a product that can generate income repeatedly without direct hourly work.

Pricing

$50-500+ per course

Package your expertise into courses on Teachable, Udemy, or your own platform. Courses sell for $50-500+. Create once, sell repeatedly. Summer months are perfect for recording. Teachers' skills in breaking down concepts translate directly.

#### Pros

- Passive income potential
- Scalable business model
- Leverages teaching skills for content
- High profit margins once created

#### Cons

- Significant upfront time investment
- Requires marketing and promotion
- Platform fees can reduce earnings

Our Verdict

This idea offers excellent scalability and passive income potential. Focus on a niche where your expertise shines and create high-quality content. Summer is ideal for development, with sales continuing throughout the year.

90 free credits • No card required

3

### 3\. Teachers Pay Teachers

Best for: Teachers who have a wealth of self-created lesson plans and resources and want to monetize them.

Pricing

Sales of lesson plans/resources

Sell lesson plans, worksheets, and classroom resources to other teachers. Top sellers earn $50,000+ annually. Your existing materials can generate passive income. Focus on your specialty subjects and grade levels.

#### Pros

- Utilize existing classroom materials
- Passive income stream
- Low startup cost
- Direct market to fellow educators

#### Cons

- Marketplace competition is high
- Requires consistent content creation
- Platform takes a percentage of sales

Our Verdict

An accessible way to earn from materials you've already developed. Start by uploading your best resources and actively engage with the platform's community. Consistency in adding new, high-quality content is key to significant earnings.

90 free credits • No card required

4

### 4\. Educational Consulting

Best for: Experienced teachers or administrators looking to apply their expertise to schools, districts, or edtech companies on a project basis.

Pricing

$500-1,500 day rates

Consult for schools, districts, or edtech companies. Curriculum development, professional development, program evaluation. Day rates: $500-1,500. Summer and leave time allow for longer contracts.

#### Pros

- High day rates ($500-1,500)
- Leverages leadership & curriculum skills
- Impactful work on a larger scale
- Flexible project-based work

#### Cons

- Requires strong networking
- Projects can be intermittent
- Demands high-level expertise

Our Verdict

This is a high-value opportunity for seasoned educators. Build a strong professional network and showcase your specialized skills. Summer and sabbatical periods are perfect for taking on longer, more intensive contracts.

90 free credits • No card required

5

### 5\. Test Prep Services

Best for: Teachers with expertise in standardized tests (SAT, ACT, GRE) who can command premium rates for specialized instruction.

Pricing

$75-150/hour

Specialize in SAT, ACT, GRE, or other standardized tests. Higher rates than general tutoring: $75-150/hour. Small group sessions scale your time. Develop your own prep program for even better margins.

#### Pros

- Higher hourly rates ($75-150+)
- Strong demand for specialized tests
- Potential for small group sessions
- Can develop proprietary programs

#### Cons

- Requires specific test knowledge
- Peak seasons can be demanding
- Competition with larger companies

Our Verdict

A lucrative niche within tutoring, offering higher rates due to specialization. Consider developing your own unique prep materials to stand out. Small group sessions can significantly boost your income and efficiency.

90 free credits • No card required

## More Options

6

### 6\. Educational Blog/Podcast

Create content for teachers or parents. Monetize through ads, sponsorships, and products. Takes time to build but can generate significant passive income. Your classroom stories and expertise resonate.

7

### 7\. Homeschool Curriculum

Develop and sell curriculum for homeschooling families. Huge market seeking quality materials. Self-publish guides, workbooks, and lesson plans. Teachers' expertise in pedagogy is invaluable.

8

### 8\. Academic Camp Director

Run summer academic camps—STEM, writing, art, or test prep. Charge $200-500 per week per student. Partner with schools or community centers for space. Teachers are natural camp leaders.

9

### 9\. College Counseling

Help high schoolers with college applications, essays, and planning. Charge $100-250/hour or packages of $1,500-5,000. Peak season is fall, with prep starting sophomore year.

10

### 10\. Resume/Career Services

Help students and recent graduates with resumes, cover letters, and interview prep. Charge $100-300 per resume package. Your mentoring skills transfer perfectly.

11

### 11\. Educational Toy/Game Design

Design learning games, puzzles, or educational toys. Kickstarter or partner with manufacturers. Teachers understand what actually engages kids. Licensing deals possible for successful designs.

12

### 12\. Speech/Drama Coaching

Coach for public speaking, debate, theater auditions. Performance skills help students in all areas. Charge $50-100/hour. Speech and drama teachers especially suited.

13

### 13\. Writing Coach

Help students improve writing for school, college applications, or creative projects. Charge $50-100/hour for one-on-one coaching. English teachers have built-in expertise.

14

### 14\. Homeschool Co-op Teaching

Teach classes for homeschool co-ops. Paid per class or per student. Flexible scheduling often during school hours. Share specialized expertise with homeschool families.

15

### 15\. Educational YouTube Channel

Create educational videos in your subject area. Monetize through ads, sponsorships, and course sales. Teachers excel at explaining concepts clearly. Video editing skills are learnable.

16

### 16\. Substitute Teacher Agency

Start an agency connecting substitute teachers with schools. Charge placement fees or hourly markups. Your network of teacher contacts helps with recruiting.

17

### 17\. After-School Program

Create an after-school enrichment program—tutoring, arts, STEM. Partner with schools or operate independently.

Revenue

$50-200/week per student. Fills gap for working parents

18

### 18\. Classroom Furniture/Design

Design optimal learning environments. Consult on classroom setup, furniture, and organization. Teachers understand what works. Partner with schools on renovation projects.

19

### 19\. Parent Coaching

Coach parents on supporting their children's education at home. Homework strategies, communication with schools, learning differences. Charge $75-150/hour for consultations.

20

### 20\. ESL/Language Teaching

Teach English to non-native speakers online or in-person. Platforms like VIPKid or independently. Earn $20-60/hour depending on platform. Early morning and evening hours work with teaching schedule.

21

### 21\. Learning Disability Coaching

Help students with ADHD, dyslexia, and other learning differences develop strategies. Specialized training adds value. Charge $75-150/hour. Special education teachers are especially suited.

22

### 22\. Teacher Resume Services

Help teachers with resumes, cover letters, and interview prep. You understand what schools seek. Charge $150-400 per package. Serve the education niche you know.

23

### 23\. Educational App Consulting

Help edtech companies develop better products. Review apps, advise on classroom applicability, user testing. Consulting: $100-200/hour. Your classroom experience is invaluable to developers.

24

### 24\. Bookkeeping for Schools

Provide bookkeeping services to private schools, tutoring centers, or educational non-profits. Understanding education sector is an advantage. Part-time work fits school schedule.

25

### 25\. Music Lessons

Private music instruction in your instrument. Charge $40-80 per half-hour lesson. Build a studio of students. Music teachers can easily grow lesson rosters.

26

### 26\. Science Experiment Kits

Design and sell STEM activity kits for home use. Teachers know what captures students' interest. Subscription boxes or individual kits. Partner with science education brands.

27

### 27\. Teacher Coaching

Coach new or struggling teachers on classroom management, instruction, and career development. Districts sometimes fund coaching. Charge $100-200/hour for private coaching.

28

### 28\. Educational Travel

Lead educational travel experiences for students or adults. History teachers lead historical tours, language teachers lead immersion trips. Partner with travel companies or operate independently.

29

### 29\. Grant Writing

Write grant applications for schools and educational nonprofits. Your understanding of education sector is valuable. Charge $50-150/hour or percentage of grants won.

30

### 30\. Freelance Curriculum Writer

Write curriculum for textbook publishers, edtech companies, or districts. Rates: $30-75/hour or per project. Summer months allow for intensive projects. Your classroom experience informs quality curriculum.
