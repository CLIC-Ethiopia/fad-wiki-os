# EdTech Startup

#Interdisciplinary #STEAM-IE #EdTech

Educational technology (EdTech) is the combined use of computer hardware, software, and educational theory and practice to facilitate learning. It requires [[Cognitive-Science]], [[Web-Development]], and [[Lean-Startup]] principles.
